About Connectly

Connectly is a modern communication platform designed to help people connect, chat and share moments with others from anywhere in the world.

Our goal is to create a simple, fast and enjoyable communication experience while giving users meaningful control over their privacy and account.

Connectly is being designed for messaging, voice calls, video calls, groups, status updates, media sharing and more.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make communication easier, more personal and accessible. We believe technology should help people stay connected without making communication complicated.
